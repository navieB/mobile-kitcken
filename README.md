## Website Goals and Objectives
# Goals
Present a clear, modern storefront for “Mobile Kitchen” to attract visitors.
Showcase available mobile kitchen products (units/containers) with pricing.
Convert visitors into leads via Contact and Enquiry forms.
Build credibility through an About Us page explaining value and benefits.
Make booking/purchasing easy by providing prominent navigation and calls-to-action.
# Objectives 
Increase traffic from search/social by having dedicated pages: Home, Products, About, Contact, Enquiry.
Generate leads: form submissions from Contact + Enquiry pages.
Improve conversion: “Book Now” (Home) and “Buy Now” (Products) buttons drive users to the right funnel step.
Provide quick access to key info: prices, company story, and ways to reach the team.
